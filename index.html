Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Arivo Hotel Transfer.

Booking
How do I book a transfer? +

Simply click "Book a Ride" in the navigation, fill in your flight and hotel details, and submit the form. You'll receive a confirmation email within 15 minutes with your driver's details and a booking reference.

How far in advance should I book? +

We recommend booking at least 24 hours in advance to guarantee availability. However, we do accept last-minute bookings subject to driver availability — contact us via WhatsApp for urgent requests.

Can I modify or cancel my booking? +

Yes. Modifications can be made up to 4 hours before your pickup time at no charge. Cancellations made 12+ hours in advance are fully refunded. Please contact us via email or WhatsApp to make changes.

At the Airport
Where will my driver meet me? +

Your driver will be waiting at the arrivals hall holding a name board with your name clearly displayed. The exact meeting point will be included in your booking confirmation email.

What if my flight is delayed? +

No worries — we track all flights in real time. If your flight is delayed, your driver will automatically adjust their arrival time. There are no extra charges for flight delays. You don't need to call us.

How much luggage can I bring? +

Standard bookings include up to 2 large suitcases and 2 carry-on bags per person. If you have extra luggage or oversized items (surfboards, strollers, etc.), please mention it in the special requests field when booking.

Pricing & Payment
How is pricing calculated? +

Pricing is based on the airport, destination hotel, and vehicle type. All prices are fixed — no surge pricing, no hidden fees. You'll receive a full price quote in your booking confirmation before any payment is required.

When and how do I pay? +

Payment is due upon confirmation of your booking. We accept online bank transfer, credit/debit card, and cash payment to the driver. Corporate accounts can arrange monthly invoicing.

Do you offer corporate rates? +

Yes! We offer discounted corporate rates for businesses with regular transfer needs. Contact us via the Contact page or email us at welcome@arivohotel.com to discuss a corporate account package.

Airport Transfer ROI
How do I calculate the ROI on airport transfers? +

Calculating your airport transfer ROI takes five numbers: your occupied rooms per night, your average length of stay, the percentage of arriving guests who book a transfer (adoption rate), your net margin per trip, and the average number of trips per stay.

Occupied Rooms ÷ Length of Stay

× Adoption % × Margin per Trip × Trips per Stay

= Daily Transfer Revenue

Occupied rooms counts every guest sleeping in the hotel tonight — most of whom aren't arriving or departing today. Dividing by average length of stay converts nightly occupancy into nightly arrivals, the group that actually needs a transfer.

Guest Communication & Confirmation

Mobile upselling messages

Two moments to reach guests directly, each with two message styles to test against your audience.

1. Pre-Arrival Upsell Message

Best sent 3 to 5 days before the guest's check-in date.
Option ARelaxation & skipping lines

"Hi [Guest Name]! 👋 We can't wait to welcome you to [Hotel Name] in a few days. Want to skip the taxi lines and start relaxing the moment you land? ✈️ Tap here [Link] to book a private airport transfer, and we'll have a driver waiting for you. Safe travels! 🌴"

Option BShort & sweet

"Hi [Guest Name], your stay at [Hotel Name] is coming up! 🧳 Need a ride from the airport? Let us handle the traffic while you sit back. You can easily view our transfer options and book your ride here: [Link]. Let us know if you need anything! 🚗✨"

2. The Reminder Message

Best sent 24 to 48 hours before arrival as a helpful last-minute check-in.
Option AHelpful check-in

"Hi [Guest Name], you're almost here! 🎉 Just checking in to see if you have your transportation to [Hotel Name] sorted? If you'd like us to arrange a stress-free airport pickup for tomorrow, just tap [Link] or reply to this message. See you soon! 👋"

Option BFOMO / last chance

"Hi [Guest Name]! Just a quick reminder that it's not too late to book a seamless airport pickup for your arrival tomorrow. 🚕 Let us take the hassle out of your travel day. Secure your ride here: [Link] or reply "YES" to chat with our team. Safe flight! ✈️"

💡 Quick tips for mobile upselling

  • 1Keep it personal: Always use the guest's name to make it feel like a hospitality message rather than an automated marketing blast.
  • 2Clear the friction: If your booking link requires them to log in or enter their reservation number, consider replacing the link with a simple prompt like, "Reply 'YES' to this message and our concierge will set it up for you!"
  • 3Mind the timing: Avoid sending messages late at night or too early in the morning in the guest's home time zone.
